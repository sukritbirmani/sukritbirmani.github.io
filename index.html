2024 · IEEE ICICEC1ST PLACE BEST PRESENTATION

SMART WHEELCHAIR

A hands-free controller for people with severe spinal cord injuries, who often can’t use a joystick. A pressure sensor on a tube reads cheek puffs, two touch sensors pick the direction, and Arduino firmware turns those signals into motor commands.

  1. Puff to build pressure. Past the ON line, it drives.
  2. Touch L or R to turn.
  3. Let go and it stops once pressure drops below OFF.

Try it: drive to the ★ without hitting the furniture. It runs on autopilot until you take over.
